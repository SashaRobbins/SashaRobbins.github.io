About

Hello, I'm Sasha Robbins.

Game Programmer & Designer based in Reading.

A little about me.

From 2023 - 2026 I studied Games Development at Bath Spa University, designing and building game prototypes from the ground up, both inside and outside of university partaking in game jams such as the GMTK 2025 game jam.

I am proficient in Unity with C#, but I have also created some projects in Unreal with Blueprints, as well as Roblox Studio with Lua. Throughout my degree I have worked on 2D and 3D games, and also a VR prototype project. My final year group project "Light Of My Life" was developed in Unity where I was the lead and sole programmer across the full project.

When I'm not working, you'll find me spiking the ball in Volleyball or enjoying the F1 Grand Prix on the weekend.
