Builder's Defence

Role: Gameplay Programmer, Designer & 2D Art  ·  Tech: C#, Unity  ·  Team Size: 1

[Gameplay GIF or High-Res Screenshot]

Overview

Builder's Defence is a Single-player top down open tower defence game, where you build resources and defences, all to protect your HQ. Face endless waves of enemies that spawn from all directions, choosing specific defences to face off enemies that are immune to other types of defences.

Main Gameplay Mechanics

  • Random wave enemy spawns; Makes it so every wave in a new game is rarely the same, enemies are assigned a value and a list of enemies is generated based on wave number and value of enemy.
  • Wave system; Goes through list of enemies to spawn at random location around HQ, can be sped up based on game speed set by player
  • Building grid system; World grid to place buildings on, confines within world boundaries and blocks clipping of buildings
  • Building stats; Upgradable buildings that have a stat increase based on building type, either generating more resources, or doing more damage, etc.
  • Status Effects; Special towers that deal status effects to enemies, Ice tower makes enemies move and attack slower, Poison spell deals damage over time, Bomb tower deals splash damage to numerous enemies in the area.

Technical Challenges

The Grid System and Building placements was the most challenging factor, making sure the buildings were contained within the world, and that they couldn't overlap with the other buildings. Since there was no set path for the enemies to follow to get the target building, players have to plan out their defences in a full circle around the building.
