Melodic Mayhem

Role: Gameplay Programmer & Designer  ·  Tech: C#, Unity  ·  Team Size: 2

Overview

Melodic Mayhem is a demo Single-player roguelike bullet hell where music is your weapon, either by shooting out of a harp or playing Guitar Hero style minigames, you must fight through waves of enemies and bosses to get stronger and stronger. Mostly inspired by "Enter the Gungeon" and "Vampire Survivors", Melodic Mayhem takes the idea of waves of enemies mixed with unique bosses to make a thrilling endless shooter.

Main Gameplay Mechanics

  • Basic enemy minigames; Guitar Hero style minigame, hit the right keys as the notes reaches the right point to deal damage.
  • Unique boss fights; Boss fights with different phases such as projectiles and movement.
  • Roguelike upgrade selection; Basic stat upgrader after a room/boss is completed to give perks to make player stronger.

Technical Challenges

The challenge of this short prototype was the boss fights, having room detection to lock the player in the boss room, different attack phases based on how much health the boss has, and dealing with projectiles and movement styles of the boss.
