Lavanation

Role: Gameplay Programmer, Designer & 2D Art  ·  Tech: C#, Unity  ·  Team Size: 1

[Gameplay GIF or High-Res Screenshot]

Overview

Lavanation is a Single-player casual action game where you're tasked to eat resources, maintain your depleting heat core, and grow to a point where you can turn the entire world into a lava filled haven. The procedurally generated world provides 3 different biomes; Grassland, Snow, and Magma. Grassland is a neutral biome that doesn't has a steady stream of resources and no extra bonus' to the player. Snow biome is a harsh but rewarding biome that makes you lose heat quicker at the reward of growing larger much quicker. The Magma biome is the homeland of the lava creature, you can move much faster but resources are scarce. As you grow larger, you are able to take over more biomes and turn them into magma before eventually turning the whole world to magma.

Main Gameplay Mechanics

  • Procedural generated Perlin Noise world; Uses a Perlin Noise function to generate a unique world every time a new world is created, utilises Unity Tile map for efficiency of visualising the biomes
  • Dynamic updatable tile-map; Logic that allows the tile map to update whenever creature gets larger, also used at end game for animation and updating all tiles.
  • Dynamic object spawner; Objects spawned dependant on creature size, rate at which they are spawned affected by biome player is stood in.

Technical Challenges

Making a procedural world built with perlin noise, the perlin noise was used to decide the biome tile, the world made up using the Unity tile system that can get changed by the player as the game progresses. Each playthrough is an entirely new world with the perlin noise so visually each playthrough would be different.
