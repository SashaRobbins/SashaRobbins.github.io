Light Of My Life

Role: Gameplay Programmer  ·  Tech: C#, Unity  ·  Team Size: 5

Overview

A young women tries to protect her little sister from a deranged cult in a world where even the sun isn't safe. Light Of My Life is a interactive narrative game following two sisters in an apocalyptic world, manage inventory, notes, and a full dialogue and choice system as you explore the dangerous surface, facing off against plant creatures and mad cultists.

Main Gameplay Mechanics

  • Dialogue System, with cinematics, dialogue choices, and affected character dialogue, for cutscenes, interactable objects in scene or general conversations with characters.
  • Resident Evil inspired tetris-grid item inventory system, different sized items that take up varied amounts of space. Crafting menu for combining items, and a sister inventory to transfer items between them.
  • Interaction system that handles all systems within the game, pickups, notes, dialogue, moveable objects, doors, drawers, puzzles.
  • Save System with different save slots, manual and autosaving with in world save interactions

Technical Challenges

The save system was definitely the most challenging aspect of the project, having to capture all the necessary data to storage, and then to reload, or load a save to the point where the player last saved. Keeping track of the inventories, notes, progression variables and more, whilst also making sure interactions in the level also loaded to the correct state.
