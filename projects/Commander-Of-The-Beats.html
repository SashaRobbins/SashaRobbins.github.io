Commander Of The Beats

Role: Gameplay Programmer & Level Design  ·  Tech: C#, Unity  ·  Team Size: 4

Overview

Commander Of The Beats is a Single-player VR Rhythm game where you as the drummer boy must copy the beat that gets displayed. There are three different segments on the drum for which you have to hit on time with the corresponding note on the screen. Play between two game modes to decide the tempo of the beats and try and get the highest score.

Main Gameplay Mechanics

  • Real time synced rhythm player; Beats linked up to a real-time 4 beat tempo, at 60bpm, Difficulty increases to 120bpm for extra challenge. Selection of beat variants randomly chosen.
  • Time synced recorder; Listener to score player on beat timings, individual listeners for each bar for time perfect comparisons.

Technical Challenges

The biggest challenge of this prototype project was definitely the VR aspect. Its something i've never tried to do before and tying the whole game around that was both interesting and challenging, especially when so many problems arrived purely from the controllers being so unique to deal with.
