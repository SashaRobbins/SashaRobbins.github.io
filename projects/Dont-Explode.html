Don't Explode

Role: Gameplay Programmer & Designer  ·  Tech: C#, Unity  ·  Team Size: 2

[Gameplay GIF or High-Res Screenshot]

Overview

Don't Explode is a Single-player puzzle game featuring four randomly repeating puzzles; The maze - by only moving left and right, move the ball along the maze to the green exit, Simon says, a novel classic puzzle game where you repeat the colour combination to gain more time, Timer puzzle - start and stop a timer where a specific number is present anywhere on the clock. Number puzzle - Press all the numbers from 1-10.

Main Gameplay Mechanics

  • Draggable and interactive puzzles; Grabbable puzzles that could be picked up and dropped around the screen. Unique puzzles for variance and range of difficulty
  • Object spawner; Randomly chooses an object to spawn as long as it's not pre-existing at the current time of trying to spawn object

Technical Challenges

The interactive puzzles that also allowed you to move them around. Trying to make sure that if puzzles overlap that you only interact with one of them. Then there is the indiviudal puzzles themselves, while some of them are easier than others, the maze was probably the most challenging as it had all the moving parts within the puzzle object.
