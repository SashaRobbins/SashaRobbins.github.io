Ghostly Allies

Role: Gameplay Programmer, Designer & 2D Art  ·  Tech: C#, Unity  ·  Team Size: 1

Overview

Ghostly Allies is a Single-player puzzle platformer where you can't quite complete the puzzle on the first attempt. After resetting the timer a ghost like image appears, copying your exact past moves. Using this ghost, you help yourself to complete the level, each adding in new features to test your mind.

Main Gameplay Mechanics

  • Player movement recorder; Stores coordinate information in an array over a set period of time every set seconds. Updates to new recording every reset so always recording previous level attempt
  • Player movement re-player; Spawns a ghost player that mirrors previous actions, can move objects, interact with buttons, and collide with player.

Technical Challenges

The greatest challenge which was also the main mechanic of the game was the recording and playback of the player movement. Having to track all the movements of the player, and then feeding it back to copy the movements of the last attempt of the level whilst the ghost player also affecting the world in the exact way you had just played the level.
