House Of Chaos

Role: Gameplay Programmer, Designer & 2D Art  ·  Tech: C#, Unity  ·  Team Size: 2

Overview

HoC is a Single-player Strategy card game where action cards change how the game is played, timebombs, shields and reveals are just a few of the different cards that you encounter, with wild cards being the most powerful in the deck, changing the win conditions of the game such as having the most cards or stealing the most cards

Main Gameplay Mechanics

  • Full randomised deck and shuffle system for the cards; Randomly chooses a selection of cards to create a deck of 52, also shuffles the deck for true randomness..
  • Card Class system with individual actions; Variety of different cards that cause actions within the game, some causing the win condition for the game to change, others more tailored for player vs player scenarios such as Reverse Text and Reveal Hand.
  • Turn system against computer; Not a very intelligent computer, but it will take turns, pick up and place cards.

Technical Challenges

Whilst it would have been the computer you play against, I had run out of time to create an intelligent computer, and so the most technically challenging aspect was the unique cards in the deck, as they all had to be sorted based on affecting the player or the computer, as well as the entire game itself, with win conditions and rules that could be added and removed as the game went on.
