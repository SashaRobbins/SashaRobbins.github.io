Boomers Vs Zoomers

Role: Gameplay Programmer  ·  Tech: Lua, Roblox  ·  Team Size: 6

Overview

Boomers Vs Zoomers is a survival multiplayer horror, loading into a round you are either the Boomer or part of the Zoomers. The Boomer is the old man who has to defend his house from the Zoomer thieves who are trying to take items around the house. If the timer runs out or the Boomer gets all the Zoomers he wins, however if the Zoomers successfully escape with half the house value, they win.

Main Gameplay Mechanics

  • Round system with role assign; Server waits until a player requirement is met, assigns roles to players and teleports them into the map. Server logic for disconnects or death events to handle in round logic.
  • Item inventory and weight; Items that can be picked up, replaced on death, slow you down as you pick up more items, gain money from selling items.
  • Spectator Menu; Fully flushed spectator menu for lobby and dead players, Player profile thumbnail, username, current role in round, switch player buttons.

Technical Challenges

With this being my first Roblox project ive worked on I would have thought trying to learn the Lua language would have been the hardest challenge, but in reality it was the logic behind designing the systems to run on a server-client setup as the game was designed as a multiplayer game.
